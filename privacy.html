Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

The AnimeMaxxxing App is a private tool used only by the owner of the AnimeMaxxxing YouTube channel.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in page, it reads the channel's own YouTube data and analytics with read-only access: videos, views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, impressions and similar numbers. It does not read any other channel's private data, and it cannot upload, change or delete anything.

Where it is kept

The data is saved only on the owner's own computers and the owner's own cloud storage, to study the channel's performance. The Google login is stored on the owner's computer only.

Sharing

The data is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
